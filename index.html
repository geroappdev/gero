    Your data, your rules

    Gero runs entirely on your own Windows PC. Your library, your metadata and your settings never leave it — no tracking, no analytics, no third parties.

    Runs on your machine

    Install it on your own Windows PC. Your videos, tags and settings stay there.

    Zero tracking

    No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies. Your viewing habits are yours alone.

    Local storage

    All videos, metadata, and settings stay on your own infrastructure.

    Built-in auth

    Password-protected access with secure session management.

    There is one exception, and we'd rather name it than hide it: if you press Check for updates, Gero asks the supporter downloads server whether a newer version exists. It sends only your update key, nothing about you or your library, and it never happens on its own. Read the privacy policy.

    How to get Gero

    Simple and fair. Pay once, use forever.

    Subscribe on Patreon

    Subscribe to our Patreon, then log in with Patreon at the supporter downloads page. Every release is there as one version folder holding the Windows build, the Android app and the browser extensions — older versions included, plus the gameplay recordings your tier unlocks.

    Once you download and install Gero, the app is yours to keep forever. Even if you cancel your subscription the next month, everything keeps working.

    The only thing you miss without an active subscription are new features and updates. Want the latest and greatest? Keep your subscription active and download updates whenever they're available.

    Roadmap

    What's done, what's coming next, and what's planned for the future.

    Video library & import Done

    Import from URLs or local files. Automatic optimization, thumbnails, and previews.

    Smart tagging & actors Done

    Auto-extract tags from sources. Organize with groups, aliases, and actor profiles.

    Theater mode Done

    TikTok/Reels-style player with swipe navigation, keyboard shortcuts, and mobile support.

    Media subscriptions Done

    Scheduled auto-downloads from your favorite sources with filtering.

    Fragments & smart splitting Done

    Auto-split videos into short clips for quick browsing.

    Local AI: motion & descriptions Done

    Scan your whole library — measure each clip's motion tempo and describe its content with a local AI model (embeddings) — so games can match footage to what's happening.

    Arcade: Follow the Beat Done

    Auto-fills a beat-synced video from your library — clips locked to the song's beat by their measured motion, matched to your chosen content.

    Arcade: Caption Creator Done

    Build a multi-clip story and let a local AI write captions that match what's on screen.

    Arcade: OnlyCams & Flamr Done

    OnlyCams — build a camming career; Flamr — swipe to rate and curate your library, Tinder-style.

    Arcade: Dare deck Done

    Six hands-off modes — choose your fate, left or right, pick a number, count your yes, last one standing, your week — played as a compilation feed over 2,000 hand-written lines. No AI model needed.

    Arcade: To cum or not to cum Done

    A night at the fair. Earn tickets clip by clip, spend them on a spin of the mystery wheel, and gamble everything on how the night ends. Challenges pull real items from your product collection.

    Arcade: Obey Done

    A timed, narrated session built from story arcs — the orders come from the screen, your clips and toys follow them.

    Android app Done

    The full interface as a real Android app, pointed at your own server. Ships with every release, next to the Windows build.

    Products & the reviewer Done

    Catalogue your toys, outfits and shoes so games can name and picture the real thing. The reviewer is mobile-first for working through a pile of them.

    Arcade: QuizTime Experimental

    Multiple-choice quiz over your library with video backgrounds. Playable now; AI question generation is an early proof-of-concept.

    Studio: record your gameplay In progress

    Turn a played session into a finished video — record any arcade game, top and tail it with intros and outros, and let it run on a schedule. Not part of the public release yet.

    Arcade: Find the Clip Planned

    Search and identify specific moments from your video collection.

    Arcade: Tell Me a Story Planned

    Narrative-driven experience built around your video library.
