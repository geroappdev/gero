    Arcade: Obey

    Obey is the guided session game in Gero's Arcade. You pick a persona, a length and an intensity, and she runs the whole thing: a scripted story told one order at a time, over a feed of clips from your library, using the toys you have catalogued as products. You don't build anything — you confirm, you wait out a countdown, and you do as you're told.

    What a session is

    A session is generated in full the moment you start it, then played back stage by stage. Nothing is improvised mid-run — the story, the props, the outcome and the running order are all decided up front, which is why a session survives a page refresh.

    1. A prep screen — "You'll need all of this" lists every outfit piece, prop and item the session will call for, so you can lay them out first
    2. The doll-up — always the first stage: she has you put the entire look on, and confirm
    3. The story — the chapters of one story arc, one order per stage
    4. The finale — three escalating beats around a hold, then the climax reveal
    5. Aftercare — a closing stage, then the report

    The report tells you how many commands you obeyed out of the total, her closing mood, and which of the two endings you got.

    The personas

    Five personas ship with Gero. Only the first is free; the rest are unlocked with coins earned by playing. Each has her own portrait, voice, tastes and intensity band.

    Persona Unlock Reaches She's into
    Mistress Vell Free Soft – Firm Dress-up, tease, worship, oral
    Domina Rae 150 coins Teasing – Harsh Denial, toys, discipline, anal
    Lady Noir 300 coins Teasing – Harsh Exposure, dress-up, tease, big toys
    The Warden 500 coins Firm – Merciless Chastity, denial, endurance, anal
    Mistress Onyx 800 coins Firm – Merciless Big toys, gape, endurance, exposure

    Each persona also has a signature woven into every session she runs, regardless of which story comes up — Vell always starts at the mirror, Rae counts every edge out loud, The Warden locks you in a cage during the doll-up and only hands the key back if she lets you finish. Personas are fixed; you cannot create or edit them.

    Profiles and setup

    Everything you earn belongs to a profile, not to your account. A profile holds its own coins, unlocked personas and bond levels, and its name is what she calls you during play. You can keep several and delete any of them to start over. Once you pick a persona, the setup sheet asks for two things:

    Setting Options Default
    How long 10m, 20m, 30m, 45m 20m
    Intensity Soft, Teasing, Firm, Harsh, Merciless The highest tier she currently allows

    The intensity list is capped by your bond with that persona, not by the persona alone. She starts one tier above her floor and allows one more tier per bond level until her own ceiling is reached.

    Naming a profile Admin

    A profile named Admin is the tester profile: every persona is unlocked, the full intensity range is available, and a Skip button appears on every stage.

    The story arcs

    Six story arcs are written into Gero. One is picked per session, weighted by how well its themes overlap the persona's interests — so The Warden keeps landing on chastity stories without ever being locked to them.

    Arc Runs on
    Dolled up & plugged Anal, dress-up, tease, toys
    The lockup Chastity and denial
    The exhibition Exposure and performance
    Good girl training Obedience and verbal work
    Stretched Big toys, gape, endurance
    At her service Service and worship

    An arc is a sequence of chapters, each with a title that shows on screen as the stage label. Every chapter position offers a few alternative orders and one is rolled per session, so the same arc plays differently each time. Arcs also carry props — a plug, a large toy — bound to one real product at the start and then referred to for the rest of the session: the plug from chapter two is the same plug she asks to see in chapter six.

    The doll-up always includes panties, a bra, a skirt or dress, makeup, lipstick and a wig; stockings, lashes, earrings, piercings and heels roll in and out. Clothing can be taken off later in the story — makeup, jewellery and the wig stay on throughout.

    The session outcome — whether she lets you finish or denies you — is decided at roughly even odds before the first stage and only revealed at the climax.

    Orders and pacing

    There are three kinds of stage:

    • Timed orders — an instruction with a countdown. You press I have it · Start when you're ready, the timer runs, and Done appears when it hits zero. The last five seconds are counted down large on screen.
    • Confirm-only stages — no timer. The doll-up and the wardrobe changes just wait for you to press Obey.
    • Choices — she asks a question and you pick an answer. These are interludes: they carry no timer and don't count toward your command total.

    Pacing is fitted to the length you chose: the build-up gets about 70% of it and the finale takes the rest. If the story doesn't fit, optional chapters are dropped first, then lower-priority ones — the core of the arc and her signature are never cut. Whatever room is left is spread evenly across the timed stages, so a 10-minute session plays the same story at its quickest and a 45-minute one plays it unhurried.

    Your answers to choice beats move her mood between soft and hard, shown on screen as "She's easing off" through "She's riled up". Mood then re-picks the next tradeable stage toward a milder or hotter alternative from the same story position.

    You get one swap per session (more with a skill), which re-rolls the current order into a sibling alternative, keeping the story and its props intact. Wardrobe stages, choices, the finale, aftercare and anything on the story's spine can't be swapped, and the button hides itself when that's the case. The Stop button is the safeword: one press ends the session immediately, with no confirmation.

    Her voice

    Every order and every praise line can be spoken aloud. Gero generates the audio per stage with Piper, a local text-to-speech engine, using the voice model assigned to that persona — Vell speaks with en_US-amy-medium, The Warden with the flat, low en_US-kathleen-low, Onyx with the Scottish en_GB-alba-medium, and so on.

    Voice needs Piper installed

    Piper and the persona voice models are not bundled with the app. Out of the box the Piper executable and voice model paths are empty, and Obey runs text-only until you point them at an installed Piper. Nothing breaks — she simply stays silent, no error is shown, and the session plays exactly the same. If a persona's assigned voice model isn't installed, Gero falls back to the first voice it can find.

    Personas can also be given an optional Ollama text model that rephrases each seeded line in her own voice before it's spoken. No persona ships with one configured, so by default she speaks the written line verbatim. If a model is set but Ollama is unreachable, the original line is used.

    The clip feed

    Behind every order, Gero plays a continuous fullscreen feed of clips from your library, chosen to fit what she's telling you to do.

    Matching works in three passes. First it looks at your videos' cached frame descriptions, ranking them by how many of the stage's keywords — the item terms she's naming, plus the stage's theme tags — appear in what the frames were described as showing. If nothing matches, it falls back to a tag and title match, and finally to any playable video.

    The previous stage's clips are pushed to the back of the queue so the background changes when the order does; if an embedding model is configured, the remaining candidates are additionally sorted most-different-first. Clips are letterboxed rather than cropped, and play muted unless you turn clip sound on.

    Products and toys

    Obey draws on your product catalogue. When the story calls for a plug, a wand or a pair of heels, Gero matches that slot against your product category names and aliases and picks a real product from the matches — with its photo, shown as a reference chip beside the order and in the prep list. She never says the product's name: the instruction always uses the generic term ("your little plug", "your wand"), and the real name only labels the chip on screen. Within a session, no product is picked twice.

    Products are optional

    If a slot matches no category, or every product in it is already in use, the order keeps its generic wording and shows no chip. The session runs fine on an empty catalogue — you just lose the photos and the "use this one" specificity.

    Settings and controls

    The options sheet is shared across every persona and profile:

    Setting Description Default
    Clip sound Play the gameplay clip with its own audio Off
    Her voice She speaks her commands aloud On
    Next stage key Rebindable key that starts or confirms an order Space
    Pause key Rebindable key that pauses and resumes Escape
    Excluded tags Videos with these tags never appear in Obey None

    Pausing stops the clip, the voice and the countdown together. Excluded tags are global — they apply to every persona and every profile.

    Coins, bond and skills

    Finishing a session pays the active profile 50 coins plus 5 per command obeyed, and adds 5 affinity plus 1 per command to your bond with that persona. Every 40 affinity is one bond level, and each level lets her go one intensity tier further.

    Coins unlock new personas and buy per-persona skills:

    Skill Cost Requires Effect
    Second thoughts 120 coins — One extra swap every session
    Sharper edge 250 coins Bond lv 1 She'll push you one tier harder
    Insatiable 500 coins Bond lv 3 She goes two tiers beyond her usual reach

    Skills are bought from the setup sheet and belong to one persona on one profile. The tier bonuses don't stack — the strongest one you own applies.
